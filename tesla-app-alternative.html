01 / The gap

The app is built to be opened. Not to reach out to you.

The Tesla app is great for what it does — lock, unlock, precondition, check state on demand. But it's fundamentally a pull interface: you open it, it tells you. It wasn't built to proactively flag the things you'd actually want to know about while you're not looking:

  • 🔌Charging interrupted overnight. Cable knocked loose, breaker tripped, station went offline — you find out in the morning, hours late.
  • 🛞Slow tyre pressure drop. Nothing prompts you to check until the dash warning is already on.
  • 🔓Car left unlocked. Easy to miss when you're not actively checking the app.
  • 💰What did this month actually cost? The app has no monthly report — kWh added, sessions, spend by tariff.
  • 📊Efficiency vs. EPA rating. No trend view of how your real-world efficiency compares over time.

None of that means the Tesla app is bad — it's just not a notification system. WattsonBot is the layer that watches and tells you, in the chat app you already have open all day.

04 / FAQ

Fair questions.

Does WattsonBot replace the Tesla app?

No. You still use the Tesla app to lock, unlock, precondition or control the car. WattsonBot adds the notification and reporting layer the app doesn't have.

Why would I need this if I already have the app?

The app tells you state when you open it. It doesn't proactively message you about a charge interrupted overnight, a slow tyre pressure drop, or send a monthly cost report — WattsonBot does, in Telegram.

Can it control or unlock my car?

No. The refresh token grants read-only access to vehicle data. WattsonBot cannot send commands to the car, and you can revoke the token at any time from your Tesla account.

Is my Tesla password shared with WattsonBot?

No. You log in on Tesla's own page; WattsonBot only ever receives a revocable refresh token.

What alerts does it send that the app doesn't?

Charge interrupted, low tyre pressure per wheel, car left unlocked, Sentry Mode toggled, drive-ended summaries with cost, and a full monthly report.

What does it cost?

$5/month for the Solo plan — one car, all alerts, all 16 commands, stats and history. Free trial included.
