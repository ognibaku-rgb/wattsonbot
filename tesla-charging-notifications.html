01 / The habit

"Is it charging yet?" Check the app to find out.

You plug in and want to know it actually started — not just that the cable clicked in. You want to know when it hits your target so you can move the car for someone else. And if a breaker trips or the cable gets bumped loose overnight, you want to find out before you're leaving on a low battery, not after.

  • 🔌Confirming it started. A quick check that the plug actually engaged, without walking back out to the car.
  • 🎯Knowing when it's done. So you can unplug, move the car, or free up a shared charger.
  • ⚠️Catching interruptions early. A tripped breaker or loose connector at 2am is better caught at 2am than at 7am.

WattsonBot watches your Tesla's charging state and messages you the moment it changes — in the same Telegram chat as your battery, tyre pressure and Sentry Mode alerts.

What it doesn't do

WattsonBot connects with a read-only refresh token — it can observe and report charging state, but it can't start, stop, or adjust the charge limit remotely; that stays in the Tesla app. It also polls on an interval rather than streaming, so alerts land within roughly half a minute of the actual event, not the instant it happens.

04 / FAQ

Fair questions.

How do I get a notification when my Tesla starts charging?

Connect your Tesla to WattsonBot with a read-only refresh token, and it sends a Telegram message about 35 seconds after you plug in and charging begins — no setup beyond linking the car.

Can I get an alert when charging reaches my target?

Yes. WattsonBot messages you when the car reaches the charge limit you've set in the Tesla app, so you know it's ready without opening anything.

Will I be notified if charging stops unexpectedly?

Yes. If charging stops before your set limit — unplugged, tripped breaker, connector fault — you get a charge-interrupted alert, so you can catch it early instead of finding a low battery in the morning.

Does this replace the Tesla app's charging notifications?

It complements it. WattsonBot delivers the same kind of event to Telegram — where many people already have a chat open — alongside battery, tyre pressure, Sentry Mode and drive alerts, plus stats and history on demand via chat commands.

Is my Tesla password shared with WattsonBot?

No. You log in on Tesla's own page; WattsonBot only ever receives a revocable, read-only refresh token — it can't start or stop charging remotely.
