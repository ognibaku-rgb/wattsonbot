01 / The trade-off

TeslaMate is brilliant. Hosting it is a chore.

TeslaMate is the best open-source Tesla data logger there is — free software, beautiful Grafana dashboards, a passionate community. But "free" assumes you run it yourself, and that part is a real, recurring job:

  • 🖥️A server. A VPS or a machine at home that stays on 24/7 — typically $5–10/month before you've received a single alert.
  • 🐳Docker Compose, PostgreSQL, MQTT. Initial setup is an evening if everything goes right — and the first debugging session when it doesn't.
  • 🔄Updates. TeslaMate tracks Tesla's API changes; skip updates and data stops flowing. Someone has to read the release notes and migrate.
  • 💾Backups. Your entire driving history lives in that database. No backups — no history the day the disk dies.
  • 🔔Alerts are DIY. Out of the box TeslaMate gives you dashboards, not push notifications. Alerts mean wiring up MQTT with Node-RED or scripts — another system to maintain.

If you enjoy that kind of tinkering — genuinely, self-host it. If you just want the alerts, that's exactly the part we productized.

04 / FAQ

Fair questions.

Is WattsonBot based on TeslaMate?

Yes — under the hood it runs the same open-source TeslaMate engine (an unmodified official release) in a private instance dedicated to you. We maintain the servers, updates and backups. Credit where due: the TeslaMate project is what makes this data possible.

Do I need a VPS or Docker?

No. Nothing to install, nothing to host. If you can open a Telegram chat, you can run WattsonBot.

Can it control or unlock my car?

No. The refresh token grants read-only access to vehicle data. WattsonBot cannot send commands to the car, and you can revoke the token at any time from your Tesla account.

Can I switch back to self-hosting later?

Anytime. There's no lock-in: cancel with /billing, revoke the token, and spin up your own TeslaMate whenever you like.

How is this different from the Tesla app?

The Tesla app shows you state when you open it. WattsonBot pushes what the app won't: charging interrupted, low tyre pressure, car left unlocked, Sentry Mode toggles, drive summaries with energy cost — without you having to check.

What does it cost?

$5/month for the Solo plan — one car, all alerts, all 16 commands, stats and history. There's a free trial, and you can cancel anytime from Telegram.
